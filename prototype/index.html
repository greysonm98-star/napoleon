Reading the map

Line. Every musket fires. It advances slowly, wheels slowly, and cavalry will roll it up from the flank.
Column. Fast, and it hits hard with the bayonet. Only the front ranks fire, and roundshot ploughs through it.
Square. Cavalry cannot break it. It barely moves, and guns tear it apart.
Cavalry. Deadly to anything shaken, changing formation or caught in flank. Blown after every charge.
Guns. Roundshot out to 430 paces, canister inside 150. Helpless once anyone reaches them.
Morale. The bar under each unit. Units break from fear long before they run out of men, and a rout shakes everyone nearby. When three fifths of an army's units have broken, the army goes.
